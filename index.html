The Method

A quiet, deliberate process. Paste a source, and watch structure emerge from the noise — concepts, relationships, and narrative, composed into a single living map.

I

Provide a Source

Paste text, drop a document, or name a topic. Anything you're learning today becomes raw material.

II

AI Reads Deeply

Key concepts, hierarchies, examples and connections are extracted and weighted for importance.

III

A Map Appears

Ideas arrange themselves into an interactive constellation you can expand, explore, and reshape.

IV

It Grows Daily

Each new resource attaches to what you already know, building an ever-deepening visual story.
