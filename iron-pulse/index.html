01 About the gym

Not just a gym.A standard.

Planet Muscle was built on one belief: results come from showing up, training with intent and being held to a higher standard. Every detail of the space is designed to help you do exactly that.

From your first visit to your hundredth session, you'll train in a focused environment with the right equipment, structured programs and coaches who care how you move — not just how much you lift.

  • Serious equipment

    Racks, platforms, free weights and conditioning tools laid out for real training — not crowded corners.

  • Coached, not left alone

    Coaches on the floor to guide technique, adjust programming and keep every session safe.

  • Built for every level

    First-timers, lifters and busy professionals train side by side with plans that fit them.

A quick, private check using the standard formula: weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

BMI is a limited screening measure for adults, not a diagnosis. It doesn't account for muscle mass, body composition, age, sex or ethnicity, and adult ranges are not appropriate for children or teenagers. For personal health advice, please speak to a doctor or qualified health professional.
