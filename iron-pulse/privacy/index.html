Who we are

This website is operated by Planet Muscle Performance & Fitness Centre (“Planet Muscle Performance & Fitness Centre”, “we”). You can contact us at hello@your-gym-domain.in or +91 00000 00000.

Information you share

This website does not have user accounts and does not store form submissions on our servers. When you use the free-visit form or an enquiry button, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is shared with us until you choose to send that message.

  • Information in WhatsApp messages you send us (such as name, phone number, preferred visit time) is used only to respond to your enquiry and arrange your visit.
  • WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

BMI calculator

The BMI calculator runs entirely in your browser. Height and weight values are not saved, transmitted or stored.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated to describe them. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance.

Third-party links

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and social media open services with their own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete enquiry messages you have sent us by contacting us using the details above.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the latest version.
